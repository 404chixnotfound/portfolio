CampusPredict

A prediction market for campus life.

Open the app ↗

Overview

CampusPredict is a decentralized prediction market for campus life, running on the Monad testnet.

Anyone can create a market, anyone can vote, and every action is on-chain and verifiable.

Highlights

  • Anyone can create a market (question + deadline).
  • Anyone can vote "Yes" or "No" — one vote per wallet.
  • The creator settles the outcome after the deadline.
  • All actions are on-chain and verifiable.

CampusPredict

A prediction market for campus life.

Open the app ↗

Overview

A decentralized prediction market for campus life, running on the Monad testnet. Ask a question, let people call it, and settle it on-chain.

Anyone can create a market, anyone can vote, and every action is on-chain and verifiable — no middleman deciding what counts.

How it works

  1. Create — anyone opens a market with a question and a deadline
  2. Vote — Yes or No, one vote per wallet
  3. Settle — the creator resolves the outcome after the deadline
  4. Verify — every action is on-chain and checkable

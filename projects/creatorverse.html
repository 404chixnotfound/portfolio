Overview

A home for creators to show what they're building, and find the people who can help finish it. Built around one idea: the project, not the profile.

Indie developers and creators post what they're building, list the exact roles they still need, and find collaborators to finish it. It replaces scattered X threads and WhatsApp groups with one searchable place to showcase work, discover builders and team up.

The problem

Indie builders share projects across X threads, WhatsApp groups and personal sites. There's no central place to showcase work, no lightweight signal of who has shipped what, and finding a co-founder or a designer usually depends on personal networks. Global tools weren't designed around the local scene.

How it works

  1. Discover — see what people are building
  2. Connect — find who you need
  3. Collaborate — agree on the work
  4. Build — ship it together
  5. Showcase — prove what you made

Highlights

Roles, not vague collabs

Projects list the specific roles they're missing. When a request for a role is accepted, the role marks itself filled.

Structured discovery

A curated keyword system — 77 tech-stack terms, 50+ skills and roles, and 6 opportunity tags — with autocomplete only, so search stays clean.

Stage badges

Idea, In Development or Completed, so people know whether you're pitching or already building.

Requests with real statuses

Pending, accepted or moved on — with an optional portfolio link and a thread for each request.

A workspace per project

Overview, Collab and Milestones tabs, with active builders shown alongside the owner.

Profiles that show work

Cover, tabs, skills from the shared vocabulary and a short bio preview.

Onboarding & moderation

A 3-step onboarding wizard, and moderator tools to curate the keyword list.

Safe by default

Row-level security in Postgres; deleting a project means typing your own username; image uploads capped at 3MB.

Design — Cyber Neon

A locked design system for a builder audience: liquid-glass surfaces, film grain, atmospheric glows and full reduced-motion support. Mobile-first, with a bottom nav and no top header for signed-in users.

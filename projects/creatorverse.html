Overview

CreatorVerse NG is built around one idea: the project, not the profile. Nigerian developers and creators post what they're building, list the exact roles they still need, and find collaborators to finish it.

It replaces scattered Twitter threads and WhatsApp groups with one searchable place to showcase work, discover builders and team up.

How it works

  1. Discover — see what people are building
  2. Connect — find who you need
  3. Collaborate — agree on the work
  4. Build — ship it together
  5. Showcase — prove what you made

Highlights

  • Projects list the specific roles they're missing; a role closes automatically once a request for it is accepted.
  • Structured discovery: a curated keyword system (tech stack, skills and roles, opportunity tags) keeps search consistent instead of free-form tags.
  • Collaboration requests have real statuses — pending, accepted or moved on — with a thread per request.
  • Sign in with email OTP or X; data protected with row-level security in Postgres.
  • A locked "Cyber Neon" design system, mobile-first, with reduced-motion support.

HangMan GO!

A classic word game, behind the screen.

Overview

A 2D indie educational video game that takes the classic word-guessing game — usually played with pen and paper — and brings it behind the screen.

HangMan GO! keeps the concept of the original game: guess the hidden word one letter at a time before you run out of tries. The difference is that it now lives in a game, with the presentation that come with one.

Highlights

The classic, reimagined

The familiar pen-and-paper word game, rebuilt as a proper 2D video game.

Educational by design

An indie educational game — word-guessing as a way to learn and practise.

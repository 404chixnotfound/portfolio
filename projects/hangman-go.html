HangMan GO!

A classic word game, behind the screen.

Overview

HangMan GO! is a 2D indie educational video game that takes the original word-guessing game — usually played with pen and paper — and brings it behind the screen.

Built from scratch with LÖVE2D and Lua, with sound handled through OpenAL.

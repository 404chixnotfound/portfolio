Melon Jelly.

A slice of summer. A little wobble.

Open the demo ↗

Overview

A thick, translucent watermelon jelly slice you can pick up, pull, twist and drop — rendered live with WebGPU and simulated as a real soft body.

Grab the tip, a corner, the flesh or the rind. Let go and it wobbles, then settles back to its shape. The look is aimed at expensive gummy candy shot in a studio: glossy rind, ruby flesh you can see into, and seeds suspended inside.

How it works

  1. Simulate — a tetrahedral XPBD soft body with volume preservation and a firmer rind
  2. Skin — a smooth render mesh is bound to the tetrahedra, with seeds riding along
  3. Render — WGSL shaders do depth absorption, refraction, Fresnel and soft contact shadows
  4. Interact — mouse and touch, with sliders for firmness and damping

Highlights

Real soft body

Stable under hard drops and stretches, with no inversion or explosions.

Genuine WebGPU

Native WGSL pipelines, with no fake fallback renderer.

Looks like candy

Translucent flesh, pale layer, glossy striped rind and individually modeled seeds.

One file

The whole experiment is a single self-contained HTML file.
